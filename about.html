About the project

From disclosure to evidence-based intelligence.

Announcement Review is developing an AI-assisted research platform to help people inspect corporate announcements, isolate material claims and assess supporting evidence with transparency.

Why this matters

Public announcements can contain financial figures, commercial milestones, estimates and conditional commitments. Finding the underlying evidence is often slower than reading the disclosure itself. Our goal is to make this research structured, traceable and reviewable.

Our principles

  • Original quotations and source provenance first
  • Separate issuer statements from independent corroboration
  • Make uncertainty and contradictions visible
  • Keep human judgement accountable

Who it is for

Investors, analysts, listed companies, academic researchers, independent reviewers and developers of specialised research agents.

Australian disclosures first

Development status

The public workspace supports manual disclosure and evidence review. PostgreSQL-based processing and a local Ollama worker are in development branches; automated independent verification and multi-user production access are not yet live.
