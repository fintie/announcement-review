From announcements to evidence.

An open, evidence-first workspace for analysing Australian company disclosures. Built for researchers, investors, independent reviewers and AI-agent participants.

For users

Use the workspace to collect a company announcement, isolate important claims, document supporting or contradictory evidence, and export a review brief.

  1. Open the review workspace.
  2. Add a disclosure by pasting text or importing a UTF-8 text file.
  3. Identify specific, falsifiable claims, dates, amounts and conditions.
  4. Record exact quotations and independent sources with dates.
  5. Mark uncertainty and export your notes for further checking.

This is not a live ASX feed or investment recommendation.

Evidence review workflow

  1. Intake: preserve issuer, original URL, publication date and exact source text.
  2. Claim extraction: identify atomic assertions, forecasts, financial figures and dependencies.
  3. Evidence retrieval: collect independent sources, timestamps and traceable quotations.
  4. Assessment: label supported, contradicted, conditional or insufficiently evidenced claims. An issuer statement alone is not independent confirmation.
  5. Human adjudication: review agent outputs, resolve disagreements and publish only appropriately qualified findings.
  6. Export: retain an audit trail of evidence and unresolved questions.

Participant quality standard

Distinguish successful file submission and format validation from substantive truth. Agreement between agents is not itself verification. Report confidence and uncertainty; never fabricate citations.

Architecture and integrations

Browser: current public workspace stores records locally in your browser. Mac Studio: an experimental Python/Ollama worker in worker/ can generate draft claim extractions on localhost. Render: an existing restricted IMD bridge is separate from the local worker.

Planned capabilities include authenticated task delivery, PDF extraction, evidence retrieval, Qdrant indexing, provenance-aware reviews and IMD agent adapters. These are roadmap items, not live services.

Privacy, security and limitations

The browser prototype uses localStorage and is unsuitable for confidential disclosures on shared devices. Do not submit personal information, secrets, unpublished price-sensitive information or material you lack permission to process. Local AI outputs are unverified drafts; independent corroboration and qualified human judgement remain necessary.

There is no automated payment, wallet action, paid task submission or verified live IMD participation in this documentation. The project is independent unless a formal partnership is separately established.
