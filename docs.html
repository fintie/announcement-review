01

Announcement Publishers

Companies, issuers, investor relations teams and authorised submitters.

What you do

Submit publicly releasable disclosures with clear issuer identity, dates, original source and supporting documents.

Submission checklist

  • Confirm you have authority to share the document
  • Include company, title, publication date and source URL
  • Identify amendments, forecasts and material conditions
  • Respond to reviewer questions and provide additional evidence

Expected output

A structured record of material claims, evidence requests and review status. Publishing is not certification of accuracy.

Available now: manual text intake in the browser workspace. Automated issuer submission and PDF upload are planned.

02

Compute & Verification Providers

GPU operators, local LLM hosts and AI agent developers.

What you do

Operate AI workers to process authorised tasks such as document parsing, claim extraction, comparison and evidence classification.

Technical requirements

  • Reliable worker runtime with supported models and task limits
  • Outbound authenticated job polling; no public Ollama port
  • Structured JSON outputs, health checks and error reporting
  • Clear separation between model output and verified evidence

Quality and security

Respect data access permissions, protect credentials, report model limitations and avoid fabricated citations. Model agreement is not independent proof.

Status: experimental Mac Studio/Ollama worker and staging job API in development. No public compute marketplace, payments or task rewards are active.

03

Human Reviewers

Analysts, financial professionals, subject-matter experts and independent reviewers.

What you do

Check extracted claims against exact quotations and independently traceable sources; challenge uncertain conclusions.

Review procedure

  1. Read the original claim in context
  2. Inspect source provenance and publication dates
  3. Classify as supported, contradicted, conditional or insufficient evidence
  4. Explain the judgement and document conflicts of interest

Review standard

Disclose uncertainty and distinguish the issuer's statement from independent corroboration. Escalate unresolved disputes rather than forcing a verdict.

Available now: manual claim labels, notes and evidence exports in the browser workspace. Formal reviewer accounts and assignments are planned.

04

Researchers

Academic researchers, investment researchers, data scientists and domain specialists.

What you do

Investigate disclosure patterns, test evidence retrieval, develop evaluation datasets and study human–AI verification workflows.

Research opportunities

  • Historical announcement and claim consistency analysis
  • Benchmarking citation accuracy and hallucination rates
  • Comparing local and distributed agent performance
  • Studying disagreement resolution and review quality

Research outputs

Reproducible methods, documented datasets where licensed, evaluation metrics and appropriately qualified research reports.

Status: collaboration and open-source contributions are welcome. Public datasets, research APIs and institutional access are not yet offered.

How the four roles work together

1. Publish

Provide authorised disclosure and source metadata.

2. Compute

Extract claims and prepare evidence research tasks.

3. Review

Check sources, conflicts and uncertainty.

4. Research

Evaluate results and improve the methodology.

These roles can overlap, but verification decisions must remain attributable and reviewable. A completed compute job is not the same as a validated claim.

Platform architecture and current availability

The public workspace currently stores manual records in browser localStorage. Experimental Python/Ollama worker code and a PostgreSQL-backed job pipeline exist in development branches, but have not been validated as a production end-to-end system. PDF ingestion, authenticated participant accounts, evidence retrieval, Qdrant indexing, payments and live IMD network participation remain planned or exploratory.

Responsible participation

Do not upload confidential or unpublished market-sensitive documents, personal data, credentials or material you cannot lawfully share. Outputs are research aids, not investment recommendations, regulatory approval or guarantees of truth. No participant compensation or IMD affiliation is implied.
