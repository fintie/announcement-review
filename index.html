Evidence handling

Keep company statements separate from independent corroboration. Majority agreement is not proof. Reviewers should record exact quotes, page references, source dates and any unresolved conditions.

Evidence supports research decisions. An issuer statement alone does not confirm that an event occurred or a forecast will be achieved.
