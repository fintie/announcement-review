Product overview

AI-powered disclosure intelligence.

Turn unstructured corporate announcements into structured claims, research tasks and evidence-linked reports.

Prototype and active development

Announcement workspace

Organise disclosures, maintain company watchlists, capture original text and record reviewer findings.

Available: manual browser-local workspace.

AI claim extraction

Identify figures, forecasts, commercial agreements, conditions and material claims for further investigation.

Development: Mac Studio Ollama worker and job pipeline.

Evidence intelligence

Planned source retrieval, historical comparisons, citations and contradiction analysis, with human review.

Planned: Qdrant and independent-source connectors.

Collaborative research

Designed for specialised analysts, independent reviewers and optional IMD agent research tasks.

Experimental: IMD bridge and task tracking.

How it works

  1. Import a public company disclosure.
  2. Extract material statements and preserve their exact context.
  3. Investigate primary and independent evidence.
  4. Record contradictions, conditions and unresolved questions.
  5. Complete human review and export a traceable report.

Automatic PDF processing and end-to-end independent verification are planned capabilities, not currently delivered services.

Technology direction

Browser application · Python FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Mac Studio / Ollama · planned Qdrant retrieval · optional IMD integrations.
